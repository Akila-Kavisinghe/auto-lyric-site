auto-lyric

A personal desktop video editor for AKILA & The Wonder Machine.

auto-lyric is a desktop application built and used by Akila Kavisinghe to create short-form music videos for the band AKILA & The Wonder Machine: syncing lyrics to audio, cutting multi-cam takes, captioning sections, and batch-exporting vertical videos.

Finished videos can be sent to the band's own TikTok accounts as inbox drafts via TikTok's official Content Posting API, to be reviewed and posted manually from the TikTok app. The app never posts publicly on its own, and it connects only to the band's own accounts.

The app is a personal tool. It is not distributed to or offered for use by the general public.
