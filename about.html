About

The band

AKILA & The Wonder Machine is the musical project of Akila Kavisinghe. The band records original songs and performs live, and shares its music primarily through short-form vertical video: lyric clips, live cuts, and behind-the-scenes footage.

Why WonderCut exists

Publishing music content consistently means producing many videos from the same pool of songs and footage. Doing that by hand in a general-purpose editor does not scale for one person. WonderCut encodes the band's whole workflow, from lyric syncing to batch rendering to delivery, so that producing a week of content takes an evening instead of a week.

Who runs it

The app and this website are built and operated by Akila Kavisinghe. It connects only to TikTok accounts that the operator has explicitly authorized through TikTok's official login flow.

Frequently asked questions

Does WonderCut post to TikTok automatically?
No. It only delivers drafts to the band's own account inboxes; every post is reviewed and published manually from the TikTok app.
Does WonderCut collect any data?
No. It runs entirely on the band's own computer, operates no servers, and stores TikTok tokens locally, encrypted. See the Privacy Policy.
